Frequently asked questions

The questions parents ask first, answered straight.

Is this safe for my child?

Yes. StockSaathi is a paper-trading simulator. There is no real money, no card on file, no PAN, no demat, and no way to deposit or withdraw rupees. The worst thing that can happen is your child learns the Indian stock market is harder than YouTubers make it look.

Does this involve real money in any way?

No. Every rupee on StockSaathi is virtual. We never ask for bank details, UPI, or card information. We have no payment integration anywhere on the site. The simulator cannot be linked to a real broker.

How is this different from a real broker like Zerodha or Groww?

Real brokers execute real trades with your real rupees and report to SEBI. StockSaathi executes simulated trades with virtual rupees, and SEBI rules for brokers do not apply because no securities change hands. We display SEBI-style risk disclaimers because the educational content discusses markets, not because we are a broker.

What happens when my child turns 18?

Nothing automatic. The account stays a paper-trading account forever. If they want a real demat account at 18, they choose a SEBI-registered broker themselves. We do not refer, recommend, or partner with any broker, and we never will.

How is data privacy handled for a minor?

We collect the minimum needed to log in: an email and a display name. No phone, no address, no PAN, no Aadhaar. Trade data stays on your device first and syncs only if you opt in. You can delete every byte from the settings page or by emailing the grievance officer. Full details on the privacy page.
